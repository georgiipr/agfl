Every new fixed recipe underperformed the original control on mean validation accuracy. Within the larger architecture, LR 0.001 was weaker than 0.005 for both losses. Cross-entropy at 0.005 also did not outperform focal loss on average. This study does not establish how cross-entropy would perform on the unchanged seven-token architecture.

The four new candidates combined 15 tokens with separate identity-initialized hop projections. Parameter count rose from 8,052 to 9,844 (+22.25%). Because these architecture changes were combined, the result cannot isolate the effect of temporal resolution from that of hop projections.

The larger LR-0.005 variants fit training data more closely but generalize worse on validation. This is consistent with overfitting. The LR-0.001 variants fit training data less well and still fail to improve validation; the files do not demonstrate that more epochs would fix them. Training accuracy is recorded with dropout active, so the train–validation gap is descriptive rather than a controlled evaluation-mode comparison.

All 25 histories contain 250 epochs, finite recorded metrics and no skipped AMP steps. Checkpoints were selected before the final epoch. For example, the winning focal capacity run on seed 2 fell from 94.44% validation at epoch 121 to 83.33% at epoch 250. The saved test result correctly uses epoch 121.

The new AGFL projections were active

The two selected capacity models have 15 tokens and 480 classifier features; the controls have seven tokens and 224 features. All 24 per-head/per-hop projection matrices in the two capacity winners moved away from identity. Saved projection heatmaps contain learned off-diagonal terms. Mean learned coefficients for hops 0/1/2 are [0.4008, 1.0662, −0.0636] for seed 1 and [0.3417, 1.0086, −0.0492] for seed 2. The initially zero self-feature term became active.

Saved features and graph values are finite, with no constant feature columns. There is no saved-artifact evidence of a dead initialization or collapsed representation. These observations cannot exclude every implementation defect.

The scheduled graph now retains 12/15 neighbors for the capacity models, compared with 5/7 for the controls. The capacity models’ mean graph entropy is 94.84% and 97.60% of the maximum over 12 neighbors. Attention remains broad on average, but the complete AGFL operator also contains learned signed coefficients and feature transforms. These graph statistics alone do not prove harmful averaging.

Decision supported by this report

Keep the original seven-token sparse recipe as the development control. Do not promote the 15-token/projection package or LR 0.001 as an improvement. Preserve this completed study and its negative result. No rerun is needed to confirm what these files already show.

Increasing capacity did not move this procedure toward 90%. Reaching a 90% mean on these same split sizes would require 243/270 correct occurrences, 15 more than now. That arithmetic is a target, not a prediction. The experiment leaves the separate effects of hop projections and pooling unresolved; any further mechanism claim needs to distinguish them, and any later AGFL superiority claim needs a matched comparison with the other attentions.

No training settings or implementation files were changed during this analysis. No project code, training, inference, checkpoint loading or project tests ran locally. The report was assembled from saved JSON/NPZ records and existing cluster-generated plots only.
